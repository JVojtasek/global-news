## What a membership is

A membership is a recurring payment that pays for the work and gets you a small
number of concrete things, listed on the membership page and in your welcome
e-mail. Today those are: long pieces sent to you a week before they open to
everyone, pieces that go out only by e-mail, a weekly selection built from
topics you choose yourself, and a site with no advertising on it.

## What a membership is not

- **It is not a login.** This site is a set of static files. There is no
  account, no password and no server that could check who you are. What you pay
  for arrives by e-mail and, in the case of early access, by being published to
  members first. We will never claim a page is locked when it is not.
- **It is not a share, an investment or a donation to a charity.** You are
  buying a subscription to journalism.
- **It is not influence over what we publish.** Partners can suggest topics and
  every suggestion gets read and answered. Nobody, at any price, can decide what
  we write, what we leave out, or how a story turns out.
- **It is not a promise that a specific article will exist.** We publish what
  passes our editorial rules. Sometimes that means a piece does not get written.

## The content is journalism, not advice

Everything here is reporting, background and reflection. It is not legal,
medical, financial, tax or investment advice, and it is not a substitute for a
professional who knows your situation. For your health, ask a doctor. For your
money, ask a licensed adviser. We say where our facts come from so you can
check them yourself.

## Price and VAT

Prices are shown in euro and include VAT where VAT applies. For consumers in the
EU, VAT is charged at the rate of the country you live in and is already
contained in the price you see — you will not be asked for anything on top of
it. If the applicable VAT rate changes, the price you see at the time of payment
is the price that counts.

## Your 14-day right to withdraw, and why we ask you to waive it

Under EU consumer law you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a contract made
online, without giving a reason.

Digital content and digital services have an exception, and this is the honest
version of it: if you want your membership to start immediately — which is the
whole point of early access — you have to ask us to begin during those 14 days
and confirm that you understand you are giving up your withdrawal right for what
has already been supplied. You do that with a tick box when you join. It is not
pre-ticked and you do not have to tick it.

- **If you tick it**, your membership starts at once and the withdrawal right
  ends for what we have already delivered.
- **If you do not tick it**, your membership starts after 14 days and you can
  withdraw at any point before then and get everything back.

Either way, write to {email} and we will confirm in writing what applies to you.

## Cancelling, and money back

Cancel whenever you like, without giving a reason. Cancellation stops the next
payment; you keep everything you have paid for until the end of the period you
paid for.

If something has gone wrong — you were charged twice, you never received what
you paid for, you cancelled and were billed anyway — write to {email} and you
get your money back. We would rather refund you than argue with you.

If we stop publishing, we stop charging, and we refund the unused part of any
period already paid.

reaches us. More on the [privacy page](../privacy/).

If these terms change in a way that affects you, we will tell you by e-mail
before the change takes effect, and you can cancel before it does.
