# You are on the list

Check your inbox — there is a message asking you to confirm the address.
Until you click the link in it, we do not send you anything. That is
deliberate: it means nobody can sign you up for a newspaper you did not
ask for.

If it has not arrived in a couple of minutes, look in the spam folder.
New newspapers land there more often than old ones do.

## What you will get

One email a week, on Saturday. The week, finished. Read it with your
coffee and you are done — there is nothing underneath.

We do not send daily. We do not send when there is nothing worth sending.
And there is no advertising in it, because there is no advertising here
at all.

## What we do with your address

We store it, the language you read in, and when you agreed. That is all.
Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom that works on the first
click, and we never ask why.

Your reading settings — how much news you want, which topics you have
muted, which country you picked, anything you ticked under health — stay
in your browser and never reach us. Not now, not later.
